# Photo-intelligence fixtures

These images are intentionally committed synthetic test fixtures for Kromora's real-pixel photo-
intelligence corpus. Six photographs were generated by the Codex built-in image-generation tool;
the two clipping cases are deterministic linear-light derivatives of the golden-hour photograph.
They are not stock images, downloaded images, or hand-drawn substitutes. The real test loads these
files through `ImageSource`, `RenderEngine`, and the Vision mask provider.

The fixture budget is capped at 5 MB total and 500 KB per JPEG. `manifest.json` records prompts,
provenance, derivation, target bands, and measured values. The generated images contain no GPS
metadata. OpenAI's Terms of Use and Service Terms were reviewed on 2026-09-19; the repository
makes no copyright claim over model output and does not represent it as human-generated.
