# Kromora branding

Kromora's source code and documentation are released under the [MIT License](LICENSE). That license
does not grant permission to use the Kromora name, logo, app icon, or other project branding to make a
modified product appear official or endorsed by the Kromora project.

The following are Kromora project branding:

- the name “Kromora” and the Kromora word mark;
- the Kromora logo and icon artwork;
- the bundled macOS app icon and its source/export variants; and
- names, artwork, and visual treatments presented as official Kromora identity.

This policy does not claim that the branding is federally registered. It simply describes the project's
intended use of its name and visual identity.

## Forks and redistributions

You may refer to Kromora as the upstream project when providing attribution. A modified fork should
choose its own product name, app icon, and visual identity, and should not suggest that it is an
official Kromora release. Unmodified redistributions may retain the original Kromora branding when
they are clearly identified as the unmodified project.

The MIT License continues to apply to the source code and other material that it covers. This branding
policy does not limit the rights granted by that license for code, including the right to create and
distribute modified versions; it governs only use of the Kromora identity.
