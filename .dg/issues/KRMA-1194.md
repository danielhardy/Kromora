## Objective
Hide the export quality slider and its row when the selected output format is lossless, rather than showing an irrelevant control.

## Context
`ExportFormat.supportsQuality` identifies JPEG and HEIF as quality-bearing formats and TIFF and PNG as lossless. `ExportFormatAccessoryView` builds the Quality row and updates its controls when format changes. The export setup is shared by single and batch workflows.

## Acceptance criteria
- Quality label, slider and value are absent for TIFF and PNG and visible and usable for JPEG and HEIF, including when changing formats while the setup is open. Layout closes the vacated space cleanly and accessibility does not expose hidden controls.
- Switching away from and back to a lossy format preserves the chosen quality; lossless export behavior and other settings remain unchanged. Verify both single and multiple-photo setup paths.
- Add focused UI/control tests for format transitions and retained quality, and follow CLAUDE.md verification guidance for AppKit/UI changes.
