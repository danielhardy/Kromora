## Objective

Measure RAW highlight-control interaction latency through the production `RenderEngine`.

## Context

`RAWHighlightHeadroomBenchmark` reports a single RGB transform timing per control, while the
`RAW_PIPELINE_INSPECTION.md` discussion states that the RGB controls cost 4–7 ms. On the 2026-11-05
run, several one-shot highlight timings were 115–302 ms, showing that the standalone timing is not a
stable estimate of warm interactive work. Existing RAW exposure p50/p95 data in `docs/TESTING.md`
does not directly measure Highlights/Shadows/Whites/Blacks/Contrast. Establish app-level latency
before treating the RGB cost comparison as evidence.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Measure first and settled RAW renders plus warm interactive ticks for Highlights, Shadows,
      Whites, Blacks, and Contrast through `RenderEngine` on representative available RAWs.
- [ ] Warm the path, collect enough samples for p50 and p95, and record decoder output requests,
      developed-source cache hits/misses, and whether ticks trigger a RAW re-develop.
- [ ] Update `docs/RAW_PIPELINE_INSPECTION.md` and `docs/TESTING.md` to distinguish single-pass
      characterization timings from app-level interaction latency and report the measured results.
- [ ] Keep this follow-up measurement-only; any product routing decision belongs in a separately
      scoped issue with semantic and visual evidence.

## Implementation notes

Use a window-independent real-engine benchmark and existing local fixtures. Do not run the
display-bound release capture. Keep RAW fixtures out of the repository; see `realworldtest/README.md`.
