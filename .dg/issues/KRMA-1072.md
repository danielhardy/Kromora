## Objective

Active panel stays at identity after a paste lands on a photo whose stored read is pending

## Context

A regression discovered while verifying KRMA-1068: photo B opens while its saved edit read is still pending. A Light-only category paste correctly updates B's retained session and durable edit, preserving B's saved Vibrance and the pasted Exposure. When the stored read later resolves, however, `AppViewModel.document` remains at identity. The next adjustment to B would then be based on the stale panel document and could overwrite the saved and pasted categories.

KRMA-1068 verifies the retained session, revision fencing, and durable result, but does not assert the active Edit panel after adoption completes. Keep the issue focused on the published active document and its next edit.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Add a deterministic regression where a category paste lands while the selected photo's stored edit read is pending.
- [ ] When stored-edit adoption resolves, the active Edit panel reflects the merged saved document and pasted category, matching the retained session.
- [ ] A subsequent edit composes on that merged document and persists without clobbering either the saved categories or pasted values.

## Implementation notes

Trace the completion path from `EditClipboardWorkflow` through stored-edit adoption in `AppViewModel`. Preserve source/document revision fencing and keep category paste scoped to the selected asset. Reuse the pending-read setup from `EditPersistenceIntegrationTests.testCategoryPasteDuringPendingStoredAdoptionSurvivesTheLateRead`.
