## Objective
Evaluate and implement RAW-aware highlight/headroom routing behind existing unified controls where it demonstrably serves their intent. Preserve RGB processing where Apple offers no equivalent; do not assume the RAW stage is intrinsically better.

## Context
CIRAWFilter defaults include boost 1, EDR 0, default gamut mapping and per-image highlight recovery support. Existing Highlights/Shadows are RGB curves; RAW recovery, boost and local tone map are different controls, not one-to-one slider equivalents. A matched-midtone flag experiment showed visibly different RAW exposure tone; it did not prove sensor highlight recovery. RGB exposure with EDR 1 did not reproduce that response in the tested crop. Consult docs/RAW_PIPELINE_INSPECTION.md and docs/rendering-experiments/raw-exposure/FOCUSED_RESULTS.md when available, and independently establish behavior on supported OS/cameras.

## Acceptance criteria
- Characterize EDR, boost, recovery, shadow boost and local tone controls and support/defaults on representative Sony and other RAWs; explicitly distinguish decoder processing from scene-linear output and tone appearance from recovered channel detail. Include fixed regions and matched midtones/local highlight comparisons with a suitable clipped-channel or bracket reference where available.
- Prototype mappings for existing Highlights/Shadows/Whites/Blacks only when semantics are defensible. Compare against existing RGB curves and higher-headroom decoder output while retaining RGB tone. Record objective and visual outcomes, defaults and performance; no speculative claim of recovery or forced one-to-one mapping.
- Ship a scoped RAW-aware mapping only where tests show a meaningful benefit and predictable slider behavior; otherwise retain RGB and document the result. Existing edits may change appearance; do not add legacy rendering solely to preserve their pixels. Preserve standard-image and mask behavior, keep all controls and document deliberate changes to neutral rendering.
- Verify preview/export, LUT interaction, dynamic range/color, capability gates, cache invalidation, p50/p95 interaction and tests per CLAUDE.md. No second editor or separate Develop panel.

## Paths
RAWDevelopSettings.swift, RAWCapabilities.swift, RenderPipeline.developedSource/applyLight, RenderEngine, LightAdjustments and rendering tests.
