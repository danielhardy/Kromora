## Objective

Stop the RAW-aware Luminance NR routing (KRMA-1175) from handing DNG sources a decoder response that is non-monotonic, or document why it is acceptable.

## Context

KRMA-1183 measured three local DNGs (Samsung SM-N960U1, iPhone 14 Pro, iPhone 17 Pro). All report Luminance NR supported with a decoder default of 0. Writing the decoder amount directly shows the same shape on all three, and the Sony ARWs do not have it: below 0.5 flat noise falls smoothly with no edge loss; exactly 0.5 is noisier than neutral (+43%, +7%, +25%); above 0.5 edge contrast drops 15–33% at 0.51 and noise reduction weakens as the amount rises to 1.0. Because the default is 0 the slider maps straight through (slider 50 → 0.5, slider 60 reduces more than slider 100). Tables are in `docs/TESTING.md` under "Additional camera families (KRMA-1183)"; reproduce with `RAWDetailRoutingBenchmark/testPrintDecoderLuminanceResponseSweep` and `KROMORA_RAW_FIXTURE_DIR` pointing at a directory of DNGs.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Choose a rule and justify it with measurements: keep Luminance NR in RGB for DNG sources, cap the decoder amount below the step, or leave as is with the reason.
- [ ] If a source-family discriminator is needed, add it without misreporting `isLuminanceNoiseReductionSupported` (the decoder panel reads it).
- [ ] Check whether the step is DNG-wide or device/profile-specific (a non-phone DNG or a high-ISO DNG would help; none was available), and whether it moves with macOS releases.
- [ ] Update `docs/TESTING.md`, `RAWDetailRouting` docs and `pixelEpoch` if render output changes; add tests proportionate to the change.

## Implementation notes

Evidence is three phone DNGs, none high-ISO. Colour NR and Sharpening conclusions are unaffected. No compatibility paths.
