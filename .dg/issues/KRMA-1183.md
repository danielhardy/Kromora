## Objective

Extend independent validation of KRMA-1175’s RAW-aware global Detail routing beyond Sony ARW files.

## Context

KRMA-1175 measures four Sony ARWs. `CIRAWFilter` support flags and defaults are read per image, with
unsupported Luminance NR left in the RGB stage, but DNG and other manufacturers’ defaults and
support were unavailable for the implementation review. The limitation is recorded in
`docs/TESTING.md` under “RAW noise reduction routing (KRMA-1175)”. This follow-up checks whether the
mapping and quality conclusions hold for other decoder families; any camera RAW fixtures remain
local-only under `realworldtest/`.

## Acceptance criteria

- [x] Inventory decoder support flags and defaults for global Luminance NR, Colour NR, and
  Sharpening on representative non-Sony RAW files, and record which controls route or stay in RGB.
  Three phone DNGs (Samsung, iPhone 14 Pro, iPhone 17 Pro): all controls supported; Luminance NR
  default 0; Luminance routes, Colour NR and Sharpening stay RGB. No other maker’s RAW was available.
- [x] On at least two additional camera RAW files, including a high-ISO sample when available,
  compare routed and RGB Luminance NR at matched flat-field noise; report edge and texture retention,
  halos, and the limits of the measurements. Done on three DNGs, no halos (overshoot 0). **No
  high-ISO sample was available**, and the DNG decoder’s Luminance NR response is non-monotonic at
  amount 0.5 (noisier than neutral at exactly 0.5; 15–33% edge loss just above). See the
  “Additional camera families” section of `docs/TESTING.md`.
- [~] Verify unsupported decoder fallback, explicit `rawDevelop` values, preview/export parity,
  cache invalidation, and interactive tick cost on the additional files; confirm no unsupported
  property writes. Everything verified on the DNGs except the unsupported fallback: all three report
  every control supported, so it is covered only by the existing synthetic-capability unit tests.
- [x] Update `docs/TESTING.md` with camera-specific observations and any justified mapping changes;
  preserve the existing Sony results and do not commit local camera originals. No mapping change was
  made: the right DNG rule is not clear from three files and needs a source-family discriminator.
  Follow-up KRMA-1184.
