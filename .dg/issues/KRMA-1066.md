## Objective

Collect real photography-workload evidence from a bounded internal TestFlight cohort before public App Store availability.

## Context and reproduction

The review recommends a small pilot after recovery fixes, restored release gates, and signed-app qualification. KRMA-1016 owns the Apple-signed archive, App Store validation, initial TestFlight installation/update, macOS 26 sandbox pass, and visual/accessibility evidence. Filing this ticket does not authorize an implementation agent to message people, upload a build, change App Store Connect access, or publish the app without the necessary user/account authorization.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Record one frozen candidate commit/version/build, Apple signing/processing outcome, and the linked KRMA-1016 qualification evidence.
- [ ] The owner selects a small cohort of photographers with varied supported RAW/raster sources, including macOS 26 and at least one 16 GB Apple Silicon Mac; invitations/access are handled with explicit owner authorization.
- [ ] Supply a concise workflow matrix: clean install, import from files/Photos/iCloud/removable media where available, cull/edit/retouch, export, cancel, quit/relaunch, and upgrade over a disposable existing library.
- [ ] Collect per-build workflow outcomes, local crash/hang information when testers choose to provide it, image-quality/export discrepancies, and actionable reproduction steps; do not introduce telemetry or collect private photo contents.
- [ ] Triage confirmed critical data-loss, crash/hang, unusable import/export, or edit-persistence defects into individual tickets and keep public release gated until resolved or explicitly decided.
- [ ] Record cohort coverage and limitations with a human go/no-go review. No response or no reported bug is not proof a workflow was exercised.
