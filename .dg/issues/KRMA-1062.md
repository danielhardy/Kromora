## Objective

Apply the bounded correction identified by KRMA-1061 and enforce a nonzero, reproducible quality floor for supported automatic hair/fibre repair.

## Context and reproduction

Start with the failure-boundary diagnosis, larger generated fixtures, and exact acceptance matrix from KRMA-1061. The existing manual Heal succeeds on two of six 96x72 hair rows while automatic Heal succeeds on none, and its allowed floor is zero. Target the identified auto-source or scale/blending defect; do not lower the quality bar or expand v1 into wires/large-object removal.

Minimal existing-corpus target: automatic Heal must pass at least the two hair rows already shown repairable by manual Heal (>=2/6), while preserving the dust/speck floors (10/12 manual Heal, 11/12 auto Heal, 10/12 Clone), manual hair floors (2/6), and the new declared larger-fixture criteria. This is a regression acceptance target, not a universal product-quality claim.

Source review: .context/2026-10-19-release-readiness-review.md (reviewed bb01e0c374fb; re-read current code because subsequent work may have landed).

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Implement the dependency-defined correction at its actual boundary; automatic source choice remains deterministic for a persisted recipe and avoids sampling the defect/other spots.
- [ ] Automatic Heal passes the named previously repairable hair rows and the dependency-defined camera-scale generated cases without visible mask/geometry misalignment.
- [ ] Raise the automatic hair shipping floor to the measured new passing count (at least 2/6); preserve existing thresholds, seeds, dust/manual/Clone floors, and full stress reporting.
- [ ] Cover repeatability, multiple spots/source contamination, normalized-coordinate scaling, and preview/export alignment at the changed boundary.
- [ ] Update RETOUCH.md with exact before/after counts and remaining limitations; no claim of real-camera sign-off until KRMA-1063 is recorded.
- [ ] If the bounded target reveals a separate larger solver problem, report the specific failing case and split a scoped blocker; do not waive the criterion or relabel supported failures as out of scope.
