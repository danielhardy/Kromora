## Objective

Follow-up to KRMA-1121 (Display P3 activation): scope HDR/EDR work on top of the extended linear
Display P3 graph, which already keeps out-of-gamut values alive between stages.

## Context

KRMA-1121 made Display P3 the canvas and delivery space and moved the engine's `CIContext` to
`extendedLinearDisplayP3`. Presentation (`MTKView`/EDR), highlight headroom, HDR-capable export
formats and soft proofing were explicitly out of scope there. Needs a product decision on the
delivery formats (e.g. 10-bit HEIC with an HDR transfer) before implementation.

## Acceptance criteria

- A written scope/ADR covering EDR canvas presentation, headroom in Light/Whites, HDR export
  formats and their previews, and the interaction with sRGB/P3 Looks.
