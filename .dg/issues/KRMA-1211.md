## Objective and acceptance criteria

- [ ] Render a bounded overscan around the visible ROI, clamp it to the source, and reuse it while the visible area remains covered.
- [ ] Tests cover pan reuse, boundary clamping, invalidation and exposure of uncovered regions without stale pixels.

## Audit context

Source: `.context/audits/2026-11-12-rendering-audit.md`, finding 15 (P2); assessed main at `c5057f84b4e5b84f032fcf57a01abe7ed2de695f`. The audit did not run code; preserve its confidence distinctions and validate hypotheses before changing behavior.

**Code-confirmed.** The preview ROI is exactly `plan.visiblePresentationRect`. Any pan immediately
uncovers the complete-frame underlay, which is a different (lower) detail level with possibly
different sharpening, so a rectangular seam follows the viewport until the next ROI lands.
**Recommendation:** render the ROI with a 15–25 % margin, clamped to the source, and reuse it
while the visible rect stays inside it. This also reduces ROI cache misses in
`processingPrefix`, whose key includes `sourceROI`.
