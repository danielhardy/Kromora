## Objective
Preserve as much useful color and saturated-highlight information as practical through RAW development, and route unified global color adjustments through the RAW decoder where an available capability serves the same photographic intent and improves results. Keep creative adjustments in high-precision RGB where there is no suitable RAW equivalent.

## Context
The user suspects RAW development has more room for color adjustment and requests a dedicated investigation and implementation. Global RAW temperature and tint already use CIRAWFilter neutralTemperature/neutralTint; standard-image and local white balance use the shared RGB graph. Saturation, vibrance, mixer and color grading currently run on developed RGB. CIRAWFilter has gamut mapping, EDR, white balance, highlight recovery and tone controls, but no directly equivalent saturation, vibrance, HSL mixer or grading controls in the inspected SDK. Its default gamut mapping and tone response can affect information before the RGB stages. Extended-linear Display P3 and float intermediates preserve useful range but do not guarantee that earlier decoder processing or later LUTs preserve all color information. Do not presume RAW-stage adjustments, disabling gamut mapping, or increasing EDR automatically improves fidelity.

KRMA-1174 establishes RAW exposure behavior; KRMA-1176 investigates highlight/headroom policy. Coordinate with those outcomes to avoid conflicting decoder configurations. KRMA-1178 evaluates Look placement. Prior local investigation is in docs/RAW_PIPELINE_INSPECTION.md and docs/rendering-experiments/raw-exposure/ when present; these may be untracked, so independently verify claims and preserve the relevant evidence in the issue.

## Acceptance criteria
- Inventory the existing global Color and local color controls and the macOS 26+ CIRAWFilter capabilities and per-image support/defaults. Confirm global RAW white balance remains decoder-based, its as-shot/reset semantics and saved overrides are retained, and local/standard-image behavior is unchanged. Identify which controls have no defensible decoder equivalent and remain in RGB.
- On representative RAWs, especially Sony ARW, compare decoder default against controlled gamut-mapping and EDR settings, holding exposure, WB, recovery, boost and lens correction fixed; explicitly record actual decoder versions and support. Include saturated bright subjects and neutral/color reference regions where available. Compare in extended-linear float before delivery conversion and after an identical output transform; use matched exposure/brightness, fixed regions, channel and out-of-gamut counts, color/texture inspection, and report transforms and limitations. Distinguish genuinely retained information from increased brightness, color remapping or a different tone curve. Do not infer sensor clipping from an RGB boundary alone.
- Evaluate downstream Saturation, Vibrance, mixer and grading across the decoder variants, with a Look disabled and with representative Looks enabled. Assess highlight hue shifts, clipping, color appearance and consistency with non-RAW sources. Separately evaluate extreme decoder WB and the existing RAW WB path. Do not claim the tested output reproduces Lightroom or that a larger numeric range alone is better.
- Implement a capability-aware RAW configuration or adjustment mapping only where supported by the comparison and where it serves the existing control's intent. Keep all controls and the unified UI; avoid stacking RAW and RGB operations unintentionally. Explicitly document any control retained in RGB and why. No separate Develop panel, decoder reimplementation or third-party dependency.
- Align decoder behavior with KRMA-1174 and KRMA-1176. Existing edits may change appearance; no legacy-rendering path or migration is needed solely to preserve their pixels. Keep stored documents readable and RAW develop overrides, undo/reset/copy/paste, comparison and package reopen coherent. Document changes to untouched RAW rendering. Ensure cache identity covers every effective decoder setting; do not assume a named decoder version is selected merely because it is listed as supported.
- Add meaningful tests for per-camera capability gating, non-RAW and masked behavior, persistence, graph order, preview/export parity and cache invalidation. Measure actual interactive RenderEngine cost and memory for any new decoder-stage slider behavior, and run applicable affected tests plus required fast/serial lanes per CLAUDE.md. Document both measurable benefit and unresolved limits.

### Comment — claude @ 2026-11-05T14:09:50.336Z

Implemented in 66036a80 as an evidence-backed no-routing decision. Global RAW white balance stays decoder-based (as-shot/reset = nil, saved overrides kept). Saturation, Vibrance, mixer and grading stay RGB, since CIRAWFilter has no equivalent. Decoder gamut mapping and EDR stay at decoder defaults (explicit stored values still honoured). pixelEpoch unchanged (42); untouched RAW rendering is unchanged. Evidence (RAWColorHeadroomBenchmark, 8 local RAWs incl. 5 Sony ARW, 3 DNG, decoder v8, extended-linear Display P3 float): gamut off only adds slightly negative near-black channels (most negative -0.02; delivered diff mean 1.8-2.5/255) and leaves saturated subjects unchanged; at 2500K it makes up to 72% of a frame negative (and 78% above 1 on a ProRAW), so default-on is protective. EDR adds >1 values that delivery clamps, no midtone-colour change. The decoder variant barely changes Saturation/Vibrance/mixer/grading downstream, with Looks off or on. Finding for a possible follow-up: RGB Saturation +50/+100 rails 0.33-0.67 of saturated-region channels at delivery on several files (Vibrance stays <=0.014 except Samsung), independent of RAW. Tests: RAWColorRoutingTests (fast, 11 pass), RAWColorRoutingRenderTests (optional, 5 pass on local RAWs), RAWColorHeadroomBenchmark and ci-tests.sh optional filter; lane audit verify passes. No decoder-stage slider was added so there is no new interactive cost; colour ticks are RGB and make 0 decoder output requests after the first frame. Fast/serial lanes were not run: only tests, docs and a script filter changed, no shared model or render code. Limits: debug build, single run, no bracketed ground truth, no cross-decoder-version comparison. Details in docs/RAW_PIPELINE_INSPECTION.md (KRMA-1179 section).
