## Objective

Add Vivid Slide (high saturation, firm contrast) and Soft Slide (gentler contrast, flatter shoulder, saturated greens, flattering skin) using the foundation generator.

## Context

Research and parameter table: docs/FILM_SIMULATION_LOOKS_RESEARCH.md (read first). These are best-guess approximations from public qualitative descriptions; there is no reference to verify against, so no acceptance criterion may require matching a camera, a reference photo, or the parameter table within a tolerance. Naming rule: no Fujifilm or film-stock trademarks in Look names, manifest, inspector text, test names, or code identifiers; use the original names from the research doc. Never derive from Fuji JPEG/RAW pairs. Calibrate by eye against the neutral RAW develop of Sony ARW and Apple DNG/HEIC files in the gitignored realworldtest/ folder (Apple/Sony JPEGs already have baked-in looks; secondary check only).

Depends on the foundation generator. Vivid Slide: strong S curve, global saturation about 1.35, extra in dark greens, dark blues and reds, blues toward magenta, yellow toward orange, heavy chromeDepth. Soft Slide: softer S, flattened shoulder, firm shadows, saturation about 1.10, dark greens higher, fair skin slightly less rosy. Category: Slide.

## Acceptance criteria

- Color space: all cubes are Display P3-authored (ADR-KRMA-1115, active since KRMA-1121; `WorkingSpace.current`) and stamped `KROMORA_AUTHORING_SPACE: displayP3` by the KRMA-1103 generator. Hue-sector, saturation and no-clip checks apply to the P3 gamut, so P3 primaries are in range and must not clip.
- Both cubes bundled with manifest provenance and original names only.
- Tests assert relative ordering (Vivid Slide more saturated and higher contrast than Clean Slide; Soft Slide lower contrast than Clean Slide) and monotonic tone curves.
- Saturated reds and blues on the chart do not clip.
- Values are best guesses; no photo-matching criterion.
