## Objective

Add Field Report (documentary: firm shadows, soft highlights, restrained color), Portrait Soft and Portrait Crisp (negative-style low and moderate contrast).

## Context

Research and parameter table: docs/FILM_SIMULATION_LOOKS_RESEARCH.md (read first). These are best-guess approximations from public qualitative descriptions; there is no reference to verify against, so no acceptance criterion may require matching a camera, a reference photo, or the parameter table within a tolerance. Naming rule: no Fujifilm or film-stock trademarks in Look names, manifest, inspector text, test names, or code identifiers; use the original names from the research doc. Never derive from Fuji JPEG/RAW pairs. Calibrate by eye against the neutral RAW develop of Sony ARW and Apple DNG/HEIC files in the gitignored realworldtest/ folder (Apple/Sony JPEGs already have baked-in looks; secondary check only).

Depends on the foundation generator. Field Report: global saturation about 0.78, yellows and yellow-greens lowest (about 0.6), dark blues and purples about 0.7, blues toward cyan about 12 degrees, slight cool shadow tint. Portrait Soft: very low slope, saturation about 0.85, minimal hue shift. Portrait Crisp: same hues, steeper slope, slightly richer darks. Categories: Negative for the portrait pair; Field Report in a fitting existing or new category.

## Acceptance criteria

- Three cubes bundled with provenance and original names.
- Tests assert Field Report is less saturated than Clean Slide with yellows reduced most, and Portrait Crisp has a steeper slope than Portrait Soft with matching hues.
- Skin-hue patches stay within the skinGuard limit.
- Best-guess values; no photo-matching criterion.
