### Comment — claude @ 2026-10-21T15:21:34.521Z

Root cause: renderer tone matching, not the source picker. Heal's pull/push correction is additive; the manual source at +220 px is ~2x brighter than the destination ring, so the source's own shadow structure was offset below black (healed pixels reached 0 where the clean reference is ~86 summed RGB). Fix in commit 07c5c12d: retouchApplyCorrection lets a pixel lose at most 65% of its value; ordinary corrections are unchanged. Reproduced at native 8064x6048 from the preserved damaged.png and saved revision: min summed RGB in the repair was 0 before, 65 after (clean 86); the dark blob is gone, only a faint foliage-like mark remains. Local comparison: artifacts/retouch-review-2026-10-20/krma-1077/04-foliage-dust-manual-heal-fixed.png (ignored, local). Tests: new RetouchQualityEvaluationTests regression (fails at 0 without the fix, passes with it); shipping floors, camera-scale floors, RenderPipelineTests and MetalKernelParityTests all pass. The metallib and sha256 were regenerated with scripts/build-metal-libraries.sh. Historical human Fail is unchanged; a new scoped visual verdict on the 04-foliage-dust manual Heal crop is requested.
