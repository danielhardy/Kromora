## Objective

Record and propagate the product owner's accepted Display P3 policies so implementers do not guess.

## Context

Part of Display P3 epic KRMA-1114. On 2026-10-25 the product owner approved: Display P3 preview and
all rendered exports; explicit Look authoring spaces with P3-native bundled/new Looks; and allowing
prerelease library resets instead of preserving old edit appearances. This replaces the earlier
sRGB-default/old-library-stability proposal.

The accepted ADR is .dg/decisions/ADR-KRMA-1115-display-p3-export-look-and-saved-edit-policy.md.
This ticket changes policy and documentation only. WorkingSpace.current stays .sRGB until KRMA-1121.

## Decisions recorded

1. Preview and every rendered delivery use Display P3 after the flip, with matching ICC profiles.
   No export-only sRGB/P3 choice or sRGB Share/Copy/Photos exception. Original bundle bytes stay
   untouched. A future sRGB delivery mode requires a matching preview.
2. Honor each Look's authoring space; missing stamp assumes sRGB, unknown/malformed declarations
   fail. Regenerate/review bundled Looks for P3; new saved/derived Looks sample/fit and stamp P3.
   Preserve imported/embedded bytes. An sRGB Look may constrain wide-gamut colors.
3. Prerelease libraries and saved edits may be reset if simpler. No old-package appearance guarantee,
   legacy renderer, or last-rendered-space document field; caches/frames still include space.
