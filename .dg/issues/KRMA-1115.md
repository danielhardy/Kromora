## Objective

Record the three product decisions every other P3 ticket depends on, so implementers do not guess.

## Context

Part of the Display P3 epic KRMA-1114 (read its Context first). Until the final flip ticket lands, WorkingSpace.current stays .sRGB and this ticket must be behavior-neutral: sRGB renders, exports and cache behavior stay identical, and existing tests pass. Zero-opt-out Swift 6 rules apply (see CLAUDE.md). Run the deterministic lanes (scripts/ci-tests.sh fast, then serial for Core Image tests), not the display-bound capture harness.

Current facts: the canvas and export are sRGB by decision for the Instagram-first MVP (docs/ENGINEERING_GUIDE.md, Presentation frames and freshness). Starter, user-saved and derived Looks are authored in sRGB; CubeLUT.makeFilter interpolates in WorkingSpace.current. Packages store edit revisions and embedded Look bytes (docs/STORAGE_POLICY.md).

## Decisions to record (as an ADR under .dg/decisions and a short section in docs/ENGINEERING_GUIDE.md)

1. Export: default tag (P3 vs sRGB), whether the export panel offers a choice, and behavior of Share, Copy, Edit in External Editor and the original-plus-settings bundle per format (JPEG, HEIC, TIFF, PNG).
2. Looks: recommended approach is to stamp each cube with its authoring space and interpolate it in that space, so existing sRGB Looks look the same in a P3 app; confirm or choose conversion instead. Say how a Look without a stamp is treated (assume sRGB).
3. Saved edits: confirm no saved edit may change appearance on upgrade, and whether the document records the space it was last rendered in.

## Acceptance criteria

- ADR written with the chosen option and rejected alternatives for each of the three decisions.
- Dependent tickets listed in the ADR are updated if a decision changes their scope.

## Out of scope

Any code change. HDR/EDR.
