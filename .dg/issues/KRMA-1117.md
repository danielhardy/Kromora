## Objective

Interpret every Look in its authoring space and prepare P3-native bundled, saved, and derived Looks
for activation with KRMA-1121, per the accepted ADR-KRMA-1115.

## Context

Part of Display P3 epic KRMA-1114. Preparation preserves current sRGB output and keeps
WorkingSpace.current at .sRGB. Run scripts/ci-tests.sh fast, then serial for Core Image tests; do not
run the display-bound capture harness. Follow CLAUDE.md's Swift 6 rules.

Touch CubeLUT, LUTFilterCache, BundledLookLibrary/provenance, LookLUTConverter, RecipeExtractor,
and EmbeddedLookLoader/package round trips. Parse an optional authoring-space comment header;
missing means sRGB, but unknown or malformed declarations fail. Authoring space is Look metadata,
not the canvas/export target. Do not automatically rewrite or re-fit imported files.

Prepare reproducible P3 starter assets from defined procedures/parameters (add generator sources if
needed) and visually review their appearance. Update manifest notes/provenance, coordinating the
film-Look generator in KRMA-1103. Keep currently active bundled assets unchanged during preparation;
KRMA-1121 activates the P3 set. Do not just retag sRGB tables as P3.

Save as Look and Derive must sample/fit and stamp the actual authoring space: sRGB during
preparation, P3 after the flip. Thread the selected space through Derive's neutral RAW baseline,
measurement buffers and fit so none remains silently pinned to sRGB in the P3 release.

## Acceptance criteria

- Parser reads/writes a documented authoring-space header. Missing means sRGB; unknown/malformed
  declarations produce clear errors.
- makeFilter and filter cache identity use the cube's authoring space, not the export target.
- Unstamped/sRGB imports retain their defined sRGB interpretation under either explicit target;
  imported files are untouched and embedded copies preserve exact bytes/header.
- Save as Look and Derive support explicit P3 sampling/fitting and stamp P3 in tests; default
  preparation output remains sRGB until KRMA-1121 activates P3.
- Reproducible P3 bundled assets, accurate provenance, and visual-review evidence are prepared;
  neutral/ramp tests check unintended clipping. Active sRGB assets are unchanged until the flip.
- Tests cover stamp errors, P3 Save/Derive/embed/load round trips, and space-distinct cache keys.
- docs/LOOKS.md documents exact header syntax and the bounded-gamut limitation of sRGB Looks.

## Out of scope

Flipping WorkingSpace.current or activating P3 assets early. Rewriting imported Looks. Preserving
prerelease bundled bytes or saved-edit appearance. HDR/EDR.
