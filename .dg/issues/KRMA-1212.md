## Objective and acceptance criteria

- [ ] Drive crossfade progression from display timing rather than a fixed 16 ms sleeping loop, using elapsed timestamps and honoring Reduce Motion.
- [ ] Tests cover progression, completion, cancellation and lifecycle cleanup at different refresh intervals.

## Audit context

Source: `.context/audits/2026-11-12-rendering-audit.md`, finding 16 (P2); assessed main at `c5057f84b4e5b84f032fcf57a01abe7ed2de695f`. The audit did not run code; preserve its confidence distinctions and validate hypotheses before changing behavior.

**Code-confirmed.** `beginCrossfade` loops on `Task.sleep(for: .milliseconds(16))`
(PreviewSurface.swift:464). That is not tied to vsync and runs at less than half rate on
120 Hz ProMotion displays, so the fade steps unevenly. **Recommendation:** drive the transition
from `NSView.displayLink(target:selector:)` (`CADisplayLink`, macOS 14+) and compute alpha from the
link's target timestamp.
