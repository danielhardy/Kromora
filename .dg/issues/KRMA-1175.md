## Objective
Make the existing unified global capture-detail controls format-aware: for RAW images use suitable supported CIRAWFilter luminance/color noise reduction and sharpness/detail controls where they improve photographic quality, keeping RGB controls for standard images and local masks. Avoid unintended double processing; existing edits may change appearance.

## Context
RAWDevelopSettings already exposes gated per-image decoder noise reduction and sharpness. Current RenderPipeline.applyDetailControls adds RGB CINoiseReduction and CIUnsharpMask after Apple's defaults. ISO 2000 Sony DSC00324.ARW had decoder luminance NR 0.712 and sharpness 0.890; changing either yielded measurable crop differences, but the quick test did not match noise/detail levels and does not prove superiority. See docs/rendering-experiments/raw-exposure/FOCUSED_RESULTS.md if available. Do not treat RAW parameters as mathematically equivalent to RGB ones, or conflate chroma and luminance NR.

## Acceptance criteria
- Inventory each existing global detail subcontrol and decoder support/default per camera; design a stable meaningful mapping (including radius, masking, detail/contrast), with unsupported/non-equivalent functionality still available and non-RAW/local adjustments preserved. Explicitly account for Apple's per-image defaults and avoid unintended stacking.
- Use mapped supported RAW controls where justified. Existing edits may change appearance; do not add a legacy-rendering path solely to preserve their pixels. Keep documents readable, and handle explicit rawDevelop values, reset, undo, copy/paste and persistence coherently.
- Compare RAW and RGB paths at matched noise reduction/edge enhancement on at least two representative RAWs, one high ISO where locally available; report crops, flat-field noise, edge/texture retention and halos. Document when a control is retained in RGB because the RAW alternative does not serve the same intent. Verify no unsupported-property writes.
- Test preview/export parity, cache invalidation, slider responsiveness and actual engine cost; avoid unnecessary re-development or stale output. Run affected tests plus required fast/serial lanes. No new panel, no changes to standard-image behavior, no OS/Intel fallback.

## Paths
RAWDevelopSettings.swift, RAWCapabilities.swift, RenderPipeline.applyDetailControls, RenderEngine interactive RAW session, EffectsAdjustments, Effects inspector, persistence and tests.
