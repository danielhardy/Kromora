## Objective

Add deterministic AppViewModel integration coverage for the editor-session retention races identified during KRMA-1060 verification.

## Context

KRMA-1060 covers clean-session eviction, dirty pinning, persistence acknowledgement ordering, durable paste bases, and stored-edit fencing through coordinator tests. The complete AppViewModel sequences where navigation overlaps a save and where a paste targets a photo during stored-edit adoption do not have direct integration tests.

Use generated package fixtures and deterministic store/source gates. Do not use an active photo library or arbitrary sleeps as proof of ordering. Relevant files include `Sources/KromoraKit/ViewModels/AppViewModel.swift`, `Sources/KromoraKit/ViewModels/EditorDocumentCoordinator.swift`, `Sources/KromoraKit/ViewModels/SourceSessionCoordinator.swift`, `Sources/KromoraKit/ViewModels/EditClipboardCoordinator.swift`, `Tests/KromoraKitTests/EditPersistenceIntegrationTests.swift`, and `Tests/KromoraKitTests/StoredEditAdoptionTests.swift`.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] A deterministic AppViewModel test edits photo A, holds its save, navigates to photo B, and proves A remains resident while dirty, its late acknowledgement cannot change B, and the durable edit for A remains correct.
- [ ] A deterministic AppViewModel test begins loading a cold or evicted photo, applies a category-limited paste while stored-edit adoption is pending, and proves the saved categories survive and the late read cannot replace the pasted document.
- [ ] Tests use explicit continuation or store gates and assert the session pin and revision-fence outcomes.
- [ ] Focused tests and the required fast lane pass. Run the serial lane if a production render or encoding path changes.

## Implementation notes

Use generated fixtures and deterministic gates; do not change product behavior or add test-only public API.
