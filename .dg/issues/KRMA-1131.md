## Objective

Remove the bundled starter Looks that the film-look family (KRMA-1103 to KRMA-1110) makes redundant, so the Look browser does not offer two Looks that do the same job. Backlog, not ready: it must wait until the replacement Looks are bundled, and the final removal list needs a human decision (see below).

## Context

Overlap review done 2026-10-26 by comparing the 13 shipped starter cubes (measured: gray-ramp luminance at 25/75%, per-channel tint, primary saturation) against the Look descriptions in KRMA-1104 to KRMA-1108 and docs/FILM_SIMULATION_LOOKS_RESEARCH.md. The new cubes do not exist yet, so the pairings below are judged from their stated parameters and must be re-checked against the generated cubes before anything is deleted.

Likely duplicates (candidates to remove):

| Existing starter | Superseded by | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Muted Film (sat about 0.8, lifted highs, slight warm) | Field Report (KRMA-1105) | Both are desaturated, firm shadows, soft highlights. Field Report is the more specific, parameterised version. |
| Honey Negative (warm amber highlights, B -0.14 at 75%) | Amber Print (KRMA-1106) | Both are warm amber negative-style Looks. Amber Print adds cool shadows and a lifted toe. |
| Soft Mono (neutral, gentle S, lum 0.29/0.71) | Plain Mono (KRMA-1108) | Both are flat neutral mono. |
| Silver Noir (neutral, hard S, lum 0.19/0.81) | Fine Mono (KRMA-1108) | Both are neutral high-contrast mono. Fine Mono also gains Yellow/Red/Green filter variants. |
| Paper Grain (neutral, flat, lifted blacks; no tint despite the name) | Plain Mono (KRMA-1108) | Same flat neutral curve family. |

Judgement calls (decide, do not assume):

- Pastel Wash (sat about 0.65, lifted) and Bleached Daylight (sat about 0.15, lifted) versus Cine Flat (KRMA-1107): same low-contrast, low-saturation territory, but Bleached Daylight is far more desaturated. Probably keep.
- Warm Slide versus Clean Slide / Soft Slide (KRMA-1103/1104): Warm Slide has a distinct warm tint, so keep it. The category merge belongs to KRMA-1110.
- Blueprint Mono and Infrared Mono have distinct curves (Infrared is bright and lifted). Keep.
- Hard Light, Evening Cinema and Ember & Cyan have no close counterpart (Street Negative's cool-shadow / warm-highlight split is hue-uneven, not orange-teal). Keep.

Risk: edits reference a Look by stable id (`kromora.starter.*`). LOOKS.md says a missing Look leaves the reference intact and renders without it. Libraries and edits from prerelease builds may be reset, but confirm what happens to an existing edit that uses a removed id, and decide whether to keep the ids as aliases to the replacement Looks.
