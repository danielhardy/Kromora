## Objective

Remove the brief white line visible while a cropped photo loads into the Edit canvas.

## Context

The user sees a white line on a cropped image for a split second when it loads in Edit. It disappears after the canvas settles. They suspect a relationship to the earlier partial-pixel edge issue, but that is only a hypothesis; determine whether the line exists in a candidate raster or is introduced during canvas presentation/compositing.

KRMA-754 traced partial edge coverage in edited library-thumbnail rasterization and filled the fractional outer-row fringe. KRMA-838 fixed a separate cropped-photo first-frame extent/fallback problem. This report is specifically about a brief white line in the Edit view while loading a crop, so inspect the actual frame sequence and do not assume either prior fix covers it. Candidate paths include cached/stored/thumbnail first frames, fractional crop extents, the final preview raster, and texture sampling at the MTKView boundary.
