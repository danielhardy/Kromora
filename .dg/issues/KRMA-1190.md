## Objective
Make the Light panel’s Highlights adjustment meaningfully affect a broader range of bright tones, including tones somewhat below the very brightest highlights. The user reports that its current effect seems concentrated at the extreme top end. Evaluate and tune the response to make the control useful on upper midtones while retaining its highlight-focused character.

## Context
`RenderPipeline.applyLight` combines Contrast, Highlights and Shadows in a five-point `CIToneCurve`: Highlights contributes 0.025 at input 0.5 and 0.15 at 0.75, with fixed endpoints. The same function is used for global and local-mask Light adjustments. The existing ramp test checks relative high-versus-low influence but does not establish a useful response in the lower part of the highlight range. This is a perceived behavior to verify, not a proven diagnosis of the curve or a request for a specific numerical mapping.

KRMA-1176 investigated RAW decoder controls and retained RGB Highlights; consult its results before considering decoder routing. Distinguish reshaping existing tones from recovering clipped sensor detail. Keep the existing unified editor and standard-image support.

## Acceptance criteria
- Characterize the current response on a linear ramp and representative bright photographic scenes, then tune Highlights in both directions so a meaningful, smoothly graduated effect extends into upper midtones below the brightest tones. Record the sampled tonal locations and before/after response so the improvement is reproducible.
- Preserve neutral identity and predictable, continuous slider behavior, highlight emphasis relative to shadows, and monotonic tone ordering across extreme combinations with Contrast and Shadows. Avoid objectionable clipping, color shifts, or abrupt transitions; retain a useful distinction from Whites.
- Verify global and local-mask behavior, standard images and RAW, preview/export consistency, and interactions with other Light controls and Looks. Existing documents remain readable; document any deliberate change in appearance of existing nonzero Highlights edits and update render/cache versioning if required.
- Add focused deterministic render tests covering positive and negative settings at multiple tonal levels, neutral identity, and extreme combined controls. Inspect representative images where available and describe the visual evidence and limitations without claiming recovery of clipped detail.
- Follow CLAUDE.md verification guidance for render changes, including the affected tests and serial lane; do not run display-bound release capture for this ordinary change.
