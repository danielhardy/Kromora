## Objective

Activate Display P3 for preview and all rendered deliveries, plus P3-native bundled/new Looks, and
prove preview/export agreement and wide-gamut preservation under accepted ADR-KRMA-1115.

## Context

Part of Display P3 epic KRMA-1114. This is the only ticket that changes the default. Preconditions:
KRMA-1115 through KRMA-1120 are done. Preparation tickets preserve current sRGB behavior; this ticket
switches WorkingSpace.current, active bundled assets, Save as Look and Derive together. Follow
CLAUDE.md; run deterministic fast, serial and identity lanes, not the display-bound capture harness.

Every rendered path uses P3: JPEG, HEIC/HEIF, TIFF, PNG, Share, Copy, Photos, external-editor TIFF,
and bundle companions. Respect input profiles; original bundle bytes remain untouched. There is no
independent export-space picker or silent sRGB delivery path. A future sRGB mode needs a matching
preview and is out of scope.

Activate the regenerated/reviewed P3 bundled Looks prepared by KRMA-1117, including any film-family
assets already shipping. New saved/derived Looks must actually sample/fit and stamp P3. Imported
Looks retain their stamped authoring space, or assume sRGB when unstamped; do not rewrite them.

Old prerelease saved edits need not render identically and libraries may be reset if simpler.
Do not implement an old renderer or last-rendered-space document field. Invalidate stale previews,
thumbnails and caches via space identity and the established pixel epoch mechanism; update fixtures
and document when a library reset is needed. Do not delete actual libraries/originals automatically.

## Acceptance criteria

- Canvas, thumbnails and every rendered delivery use Display P3 with matching encoding/profile;
  re-opened exports match the corresponding preview within documented format/precision tolerance.
- A wide-gamut image's out-of-sRGB patches survive edit, preview and every supported P3 export
  format without gamut clipping under neutral/P3 transforms. Do not require an sRGB creative Look
  to preserve every out-of-domain color.
- Bundled Looks are regenerated for P3 with recorded visual review/provenance. Save and Derive
  produce genuinely P3-sampled/fitted cubes stamped P3; imported authoring spaces remain honored.
- New-package/edit/embedded-Look round trips pass. Old-package appearance parity is not required;
  stale rendered data is rejected and any prerelease reset requirement is documented.
- Full fast, serial and identity lanes pass.
- docs/ENGINEERING_GUIDE.md, docs/LOOKS.md and docs/STORAGE_POLICY.md describe the activated policy
  and any reset procedure; follow-up recorded for HDR/EDR.

## Out of scope

Preserving prerelease library appearance, legacy renderers, dynamic space per source, an independent
sRGB export mode, HDR/EDR, and soft proofing.
