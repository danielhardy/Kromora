## Objective

Add black-and-white Looks: Fine Mono (hard lower-mid to highlights, soft into shadows) with Yellow, Red and Green filter variants, a flatter Plain Mono, and Warm Tone (sepia-style).

## Context

Research and parameter table: docs/FILM_SIMULATION_LOOKS_RESEARCH.md (read first). These are best-guess approximations from public qualitative descriptions; there is no reference to verify against, so no acceptance criterion may require matching a camera, a reference photo, or the parameter table within a tolerance. Naming rule: no Fujifilm or film-stock trademarks in Look names, manifest, inspector text, test names, or code identifiers; use the original names from the research doc. Never derive from Fuji JPEG/RAW pairs. Calibrate by eye against the neutral RAW develop of Sony ARW and Apple DNG/HEIC files in the gitignored realworldtest/ folder (Apple/Sony JPEGs already have baked-in looks; secondary check only).

Filter variants are channel-mix luma weights: Yellow darkens blue sky, Red darkens sky more and lightens foliage, Green softens foliage contrast. Plain Mono has a flatter curve holding highlights. Keep distinct from existing Monochrome starters (Soft Mono, Silver Noir, Paper Grain, Blueprint Mono, Infrared Mono) and note how each differs. No baked-in grain.

## Acceptance criteria

- Cubes bundled with provenance and original names in the Monochrome category.
- Tests assert filter luma weights on red, green and blue patches and tone curve shape; outputs are neutral (R=G=B) except Warm Tone.
- Best-guess values; no photo-matching criterion.
