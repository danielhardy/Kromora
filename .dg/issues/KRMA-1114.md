## Objective

Move Kromora from the sRGB working space to Display P3 so wide-gamut photos are shown and exported without gamut loss, as phase one toward HDR. Full HDR/EDR is explicitly out of scope here.

## Context

docs/ENGINEERING_GUIDE.md (Presentation frames and freshness) records the current sRGB implementation and accepted P3 release policy. The former Instagram-first sRGB-default decision is superseded by ADR-KRMA-1115. Local camera files all report Display P3 as their developed color space, but the measured share of pixels outside sRGB is small: 0% in two Sony ARWs, 0-4.39% in the iPhone DNGs, 1.81% in the HEIC. The gain today is real but modest; it grows with saturated iPhone subjects and a P3 display. The guide lists what must migrate together if a P3-preserving mode is added: WorkingSpace.current, derived-LUT fitting (RecipeExtractor is pinned to sRGB), LUT interpolation, frame signatures and caches, thumbnails, and export tagging, with preview/export parity tests.

Accepted policy (ADR-KRMA-1115, product owner approval 2026-10-25):
- Display P3 canvas and all rendered deliveries, including Share, Copy, Photos, external-editor
  TIFF and bundle companions. Embed matching ICC profiles; no independent export-space picker or
  sRGB shortcut. Honor input profiles and keep original bytes untouched. Future sRGB delivery must
  include a matching preview; dynamic working/output space per source is not part of this migration.
- Every Look has an authoring space. Honor recognized stamps; missing means sRGB, unknown/malformed
  declarations fail. Regenerate/review bundled Looks for P3, and sample/fit/stamp new saved/derived
  Looks in P3 after the flip. Preserve imported/embedded bytes rather than converting all imports.
  An sRGB Look may constrain wide-gamut colors through its bounded domain.
- Prerelease libraries/edits may be reset; no old-edit appearance guarantee or last-rendered-space
  document field. Keep cache/frame space identity and invalidate stale rendered data at the flip.
- Coordinate with KRMA-1103: regenerate and visually review film-Look assets already shipping, or
  target P3 from the start when that family lands after this migration.

## Acceptance criteria

- Accepted policies above are recorded in ADR-KRMA-1115, docs/ENGINEERING_GUIDE.md and docs/LOOKS.md.
- WorkingSpace.current, LUT interpolation, derived-LUT fitting, caches and signatures, thumbnails and export tagging migrate together, with preview/export parity tests.
- P3 bundled/new Looks and new-package round trips satisfy the accepted policy. Imported Look authoring spaces are honored; no old-library appearance guarantee or migration is required. Document any prerelease reset requirement and invalidate stale caches.
- A P3 out-of-sRGB test image survives neutral/P3 edit, preview and all supported rendered export formats without gamut clipping; preview/export parity and matching ICC tags are tested.
- HDR/EDR values above 1.0 are explicitly not required; their handling by Looks is recorded as a follow-up.

## Out of scope

HDR/EDR rendering, PQ/HLG output, soft proofing, hardware colorimeter validation.
