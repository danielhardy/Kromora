## Objective

Move Kromora from the sRGB working space to Display P3 so wide-gamut photos are shown and exported without gamut loss, as phase one toward HDR. Full HDR/EDR is explicitly out of scope here.

## Context

docs/ENGINEERING_GUIDE.md (Presentation frames and freshness) records the current decision: keep the color-managed sRGB canvas for the Instagram-first MVP so preview matches the default sRGB export. Local camera files all report Display P3 as their developed color space, but the measured share of pixels outside sRGB is small: 0% in two Sony ARWs, 0-4.39% in the iPhone DNGs, 1.81% in the HEIC. The gain today is real but modest; it grows with saturated iPhone subjects and a P3 display. The guide lists what must migrate together if a P3-preserving mode is added: WorkingSpace.current, derived-LUT fitting (RecipeExtractor is pinned to sRGB), LUT interpolation, frame signatures and caches, thumbnails, and export tagging, with preview/export parity tests.

Open questions to settle first:
- Export: tag P3 by default, or offer sRGB/P3 per export; what Share, Copy and Instagram-style delivery do.
- Existing Looks: bundled starter cubes, user and derived Looks are authored in sRGB. Applied in P3 they render differently; decide to re-fit/convert, stamp the authoring space on CubeLUT, or keep sRGB-authored Looks interpolating in sRGB.
- Saved edits that use a Look must not silently change appearance.
- Independent of the film-look Looks (KRMA-1103 and its family). If those ship first, regenerate the film-look cubes for P3 with the KRMA-1103 generator as part of this migration and re-check them by eye; if this ships first, the generator targets P3 from the start.

## Acceptance criteria

- Decision recorded for each open question above in docs/ENGINEERING_GUIDE.md and docs/LOOKS.md.
- WorkingSpace.current, LUT interpolation, derived-LUT fitting, caches and signatures, thumbnails and export tagging migrate together, with preview/export parity tests.
- Existing documents and saved Looks render the same as before, or an explicit migration is documented and tested.
- A P3 out-of-sRGB test image survives the edit and export path without gamut clipping.
- HDR/EDR values above 1.0 are explicitly not required; their handling by Looks is recorded as a follow-up.

## Out of scope

HDR/EDR rendering, PQ/HLG output, soft proofing, hardware colorimeter validation.
