## Objective
Fix the visible nub of slider track extending beyond the thumb at minimum and maximum. The user observed this in Noise Reduction sliders: at zero the small copper thumb sits inboard of the left end of the gray bar, making an endpoint look unreachable. The same concern applies at both edges.

## Context
`NeutralOriginSliderCell` retains AppKit's native knob travel and hit geometry, but draws a disc at 60% of the native knob size. Its custom track spans the full bar while the thumb center travels from half a native knob width inside either edge. This geometry may explain the exposed track; verify the rendered result and actual min/max values before choosing a fix. `SliderFill` handles neutral-relative fill, including bipolar and endpoint-neutral controls. KRMA-1191 separately covers double-click-to-reset behavior.

## Acceptance criteria
- At both minimum and maximum, the visible thumb and track terminate cleanly without a protruding track nub, including the Noise Reduction controls shown in the user report. Intermediate positions remain aligned and visually continuous, with no clipping or layout regression.
- Dragging and other supported input still reach the exact numeric minimum and maximum. Preserve usable native hit targets, keyboard and accessibility behavior, and avoid changing slider ranges or adjustment values merely to fix presentation.
- Verify unipolar and bipolar controls, neutral-at-either-end cases, and representative colored/gradient tracks, enabled and disabled states, and different available widths. Preserve the intended neutral marker and fill behavior.
- Add focused geometry or rendered-pixel regression coverage for both endpoints and at least one intermediate position, and visually inspect representative controls where feasible. Coordinate with the thumb hit testing in KRMA-1191 if that work has landed.
- Follow CLAUDE.md targeted testing and the required serial lane for AppKit/UI changes.
