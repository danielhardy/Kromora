## Failed behavior
The parent verification proves scheduler ordering but does not verify whether the Original render completes and presents correctly while the Kromora window is defocused. The user observation says the pane remained blank until refocus. Photo switching and leaving/re-entering Edit have also not been exercised at the UI presentation boundary.

## Expected outcome
The current photo baseline is presented without relying on a focus transition or unrelated user action, and remains correct through photo changes and Edit re-entry while adjusted preview responsiveness and other comparison modes remain intact.

## Acceptance criteria
- Add or run a reproducible presentation-level regression for Library-to-Edit entry with side-by-side enabled, photo switching, and leaving/re-entering Edit.
- Defocus and refocus the window while the Original render is pending; verify and record render completion and presentation behavior, including that the current photo is shown after refocus without another action.
- Confirm the Adjusted pane remains responsive and other comparison modes are unaffected.
- Record cause and before/after evidence without asserting an unmeasured latency target; do not run display-bound benchmarks for this ordinary fix.
