## Objective

Make Derive Look refuse, or clearly warn about, a RAW/JPG pair whose frames do not line up, instead of silently producing a plausible-looking but wrong Look.

## Context

Reported by the owner on 2026-11-02. Deriving a Look from a RAW and a JPG exported from an editor produced a nearly desaturated result: the sky went flat gray and the green tower roof lost its color. No error was shown.

Findings from reading `RecipeExtractor.derive` and the sample files (confirmed on the files, see Fixtures):

- **Not a colorspace bug.** The neutral RAW baseline, the JPG and the blurred JPG are all rendered into the authoring space (Display P3) before sampling, and the cube is stamped and applied in that space.
- **The JPG is a ~1.3x crop of the RAW frame.** The tower is about 1.3x wider in the JPG and its top sits ~5% down the frame versus ~27% in the RAW render. The aspect-ratio check (`aspectTolerance` 1%) passes because the crop keeps the aspect ratio.
- **The aligner cannot recover it.** `computeAlignment` searches only a +/-4 px integer shift, so on a 30% scale change it settles on an arbitrary offset. Every RAW color is then paired with an unrelated JPG pixel, and the per-cell averages regress toward gray.
- **No quality gate.** The best correlation score is never checked, so a bad alignment produces a normal-looking report.
- **The grade is not the cause.** The `.xmp` sidecars contain no masks or local adjustments. The failing pair's edit is global (contrast, saturation, vibrance, tone curve) plus a -12 post-crop vignette and Clarity/Dehaze/Texture. The control pair has heavier global grading (HSL, split toning) and a -16 vignette, and still derives well. Spatial stages (vignette, Clarity, Dehaze, Texture) stay outside what a cube can reproduce, as the Save-as-Look sheet already says.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] `RecipeExtractor` measures alignment quality (for example normalized cross-correlation of the best shift over the center patch) and rejects the pair with a descriptive `ExtractorError`, or surfaces a warning in the report, when it falls below a threshold. The threshold is chosen from measurements on a matched pair and on the cropped pair attached below.
- [ ] The cropped pair attached to this ticket no longer returns a normal-looking result. It fails with a message that names a likely crop, scale or mismatched frame.
- [ ] A same-frame pair still derives as before. An in-camera RAW+JPG pair must not regress.
- [ ] Decide and record whether to also estimate scale and offset so a cropped JPG can be registered, or to reject only. If registration is added, the cropped pair derives a Look whose chroma is close to the JPG's. If it is rejected only, `docs/LOOKS.md` states that the JPG must be the same uncropped frame and a global cube cannot reproduce spatial stages such as vignette or Clarity.
- [ ] Deterministic tests cover the gate with generated images, with no RAW fixture in the repo: a scaled or shifted copy is rejected and an identical frame passes. The serial Core Image lane covers it if it renders.
- [ ] The Derive report or sheet shows the alignment quality so a user can see why a result looks wrong.

## Implementation notes

- Relevant code: `RecipeExtractor.computeAlignment`, `RecipeExtractor.Options`, `ExtractorError`, `DeriveCoordinator`, and the Derive report view.
- The measurement buffers are RGBA8 at `workingLongEdge` 3000, so a coarse scale search (for example over 0.6-1.0 of the RAW extent, then refine) is affordable on a downscaled luma patch.
- Keep Core Image graph construction inside the extractor, as today.
- Derived cubes are still named `*_recipe_33_Rec709` by `DeriveCoordinator.derivedName`, although they are now stamped Display P3. Check whether to rename that suffix while touching this.

Related: KRMA-1154 covers the second Derive problem found in this session. A good pair still yields a cube that is arbitrary outside the sampled colors and posterizes other photos.
