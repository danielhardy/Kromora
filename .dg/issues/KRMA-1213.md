## Objective and acceptance criteria

- [ ] Verify premultiplied-alpha behavior and correct shader and blend factors so partial coverage and crossfade alpha do not darken edges.
- [ ] A deterministic compositing test covers translucent edge texels and several transition alpha values.

## Audit context

Source: `.context/audits/2026-11-12-rendering-audit.md`, finding 17 (P3); assessed main at `c5057f84b4e5b84f032fcf57a01abe7ed2de695f`. The audit did not run code; preserve its confidence distinctions and validate hypotheses before changing behavior.

The fragment shader multiplies only `color.a` by the uniform alpha, and the pipeline blends with
`sourceAlpha` / `oneMinusSourceAlpha`
([PreviewSurface.metal:63](../../Sources/KromoraKit/Resources/PreviewSurface.metal), PreviewSurface.swift
pipeline setup). The textures are premultiplied (Core Image output), so partially covered edge
texels are multiplied by alpha twice and darken. **Recommendation:** blend with `.one` /
`.oneMinusSourceAlpha`, and multiply all four channels by the uniform alpha in the shader.
