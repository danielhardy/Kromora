## Objective

Retouch source-pick tasks must be owned, fenced to their photo/session/operation, and drained at shutdown.

## Context

Source: audit `.context/audits/2026-11-03-code-quality-audit.md` (against `b7e46d5c`); probe source and outputs are in `.context/audits/evidence/2026-11-03/README.md`. Line numbers below are from that commit and may drift.

Dust-analysis lanes in `RetouchWorkflowCoordinator` own their tasks, but source picks launched from `endGesture` (line 179) and `nextSource` (line 417) do not. `shutdown` (line 385) drains only the analysis lanes. The gesture task calls `destination.endUndoGrouping()` after its awaited pick with no source/session token, and concurrent "next source" requests have no newest-wins fence.

Probe: begin a spot on photo A, gate the pick, switch the destination to B, shut down the coordinator, release the pick: `undoGroupEndsAtShutdown=0 undoGroupEndsAfterShutdown=["B"]`. The demonstrated effect is a late undo-group callback into B after shutdown. A cross-photo pixel write was not reproduced, but the root also lacks a central shutdown guard on document mutation/persistence, so late callbacks are not harmless.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] All source-pick tasks are tracked by the coordinator and awaited by `shutdown`.
- [ ] Each pick is bound to asset, source, session, and an invocation generation; a stale completion cannot close another photo's undo group, publish a source, mutate the document, or enqueue a save.
- [ ] Rapid "next source" requests completing out of order apply only the newest request.
- [ ] New document commands are rejected once shutdown admission closes; the destination is not kept alive by an untracked task.
- [ ] Tests: delayed pick -> navigation -> new gesture; out-of-order next-source completions; asset deletion during a pick; shutdown during a pick. No old operation affects the new photo or runs after the barrier.
- [ ] Existing `RetouchWorkflowCoordinatorTests` pass.
