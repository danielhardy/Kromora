## Objective

Keep the geometry-ROI processing margin isotropic in source pixels. `RenderEngine` currently applies `RenderPipeline.spatialSupportFraction` directly to normalized x and y coordinates in the post-geometry ROI path, while the ordinary path converts a shortest-side pixel margin into source coordinates. On wide or tall images, geometry ROI therefore processes more long-axis pixels than the spatial filters require.

## Acceptance criteria

- Convert the spatial support distance (`0.084` times the shortest full-frame side) into separate normalized x and y margins for the geometry-ROI path.
- Keep the ROI bounded to the full frame and retain enough support for the largest active spatial filter.
- Add landscape, portrait, and panorama coverage comparing geometry-ROI pixels with the matching full-export crop, and assert the planned support margin in source pixels.
