## Objective and acceptance criteria

- [ ] Reduce per-frame texture and quad-buffer allocation using a bounded, size-aware reuse strategy and/or inline Metal bytes, with safe GPU resource lifetimes.
- [ ] Tests verify reuse, bounded retention and correctness across resize and overlapping frames; document the allocation impact.

## Audit context

Source: `.context/audits/2026-11-12-rendering-audit.md`, finding 14 (P2); assessed main at `c5057f84b4e5b84f032fcf57a01abe7ed2de695f`. The audit did not run code; preserve its confidence distinctions and validate hypotheses before changing behavior.

**Code-confirmed.** Each `makeCIImage` allocates a new private texture
(`RenderEngineResources.makeProcessingTexture`), each publication allocates a new bgra8 texture and
readback buffer, and each draw allocates two `MTLBuffer`s per quad (PreviewSurface.swift `encode`).
During a drag this is tens of MB of allocation per frame. **Recommendation:** keep a small
size-keyed texture pool (or an `MTLHeap`) for engine output and presentation, and use
`setVertexBytes`/`setFragmentBytes` for the 4-vertex quad and its uniforms.
