## Objective

Add Street Negative (very high contrast, uneven hue shifts), Amber Print (amber highlights, cool shadows, lifted toe) and True Color (near-neutral with depth from luminance shifts).

## Context

Research and parameter table: docs/FILM_SIMULATION_LOOKS_RESEARCH.md (read first). These are best-guess approximations from public qualitative descriptions; there is no reference to verify against, so no acceptance criterion may require matching a camera, a reference photo, or the parameter table within a tolerance. Naming rule: no Fujifilm or film-stock trademarks in Look names, manifest, inspector text, test names, or code identifiers; use the original names from the research doc. Never derive from Fuji JPEG/RAW pairs. Calibrate by eye against the neutral RAW develop of Sony ARW and Apple DNG/HEIC files in the gitignored realworldtest/ folder (Apple/Sony JPEGs already have baked-in looks; secondary check only).

Depends on the foundation generator. Street Negative: curve varies by hue, reds about 1.2, yellows about 0.7, dark greens about 0.65, blues and purples suppressed, slight red lean, cool shadows with warm highlights. Amber Print and True Color have the thinnest public evidence and conflicting sources; treat as especially rough guesses.

## Acceptance criteria

- Three cubes bundled with provenance and original names.
- Tests assert Street Negative slope differs across a hue sweep and has the cool-shadow / warm-highlight tint; Amber Print tint direction on a gray ramp; True Color stays neutral on a gray ramp.
- Best-guess values; no photo-matching criterion.
