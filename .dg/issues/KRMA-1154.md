## Objective

Make a derived Look behave sensibly on photos whose colors the source pair never sampled, instead of posterizing them.

## Context

Reported by the owner on 2026-11-02. A Look derived from a correctly framed pair (`DSC03293`, a desert street scene) looked right on its own photo. Applied to a green pine/hawk photo, it produced blotchy, contour-line, almost solarized output: the greens turned tan and cream with hard-edged patches across the smooth bokeh background.

The Derive report for that pair read 3% coverage (about 1,080 of 35,937 cube cells received a direct sample), 200k samples, saturation 0.42x, sharpening 2.7x. The street scene covers a narrow color range (tans, road gray, teal sky) and contains almost no green.

Analysis of the derived cube (`realworldtest/DSC03293_recipe_33_Rec709.cube`, a local copy of the scratch file):

- 22.4% of the cells are exactly identity. `RecipeExtractor.buildCube` anchors every cell that neighbor-pulling does not reach.
- Neighboring cells differ wildly. The p99 step between adjacent cells is about 0.55 in a 0-1 range, the maximum is 0.77, and about 8,900 neighbor pairs jump by more than 0.15. A smooth input gradient crossing those cells comes out as bands, which is the posterized look.
- About 31% of the large jumps sit on the boundary between an identity cell and a pulled cell. The rest are between neighboring cells whose values come from very few samples.
- Greens are mapped inconsistently. In the Display P3 cube, a mid green maps to tan, a bright green to near cream, and a lime to dark brown. 34% of the green-dominant cells are exactly identity, and the rest were filled by smearing tan values in from the sampled region.

Not caused by the Display P3 migration (KRMA-1117/1121/1129). Deriving the same `DSC03293` pair with the authoring space set to sRGB and to Display P3 gave the same defect:

| Space | Coverage | Identity cells | p99 neighbor step | Max step |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| sRGB | 3.5% | 6,322 of 35,937 | 0.534 | 0.773 |
| Display P3 | 2.9% | 8,296 of 35,937 | 0.532 | 0.761 |

The migration only changed the color space of the measurement buffers. `accumulateSamples` and `buildCube` are unchanged since before it. The Look-before-sliders reorder (KRMA-1032, render pipeline v36) was not tested, but the cube is already discontinuous before it is applied.

Cause, from reading `RecipeExtractor`:

- Every directly sampled cell is trusted equally, whether it holds 1 sample or 10,000.
- Empty cells are filled by 8 iterations of neighbor averaging, which spreads sampled colors up to 8 cells into unsampled territory, and then hard-anchored to identity. There is no fade and no smoothing, so the two regimes meet at a cliff.
- Nothing smooths the cube as a whole. The result is a lookup table that fits the source photo but is arbitrary elsewhere.

This is separate from KRMA-1153 (a cropped JPG gives a bad alignment). That bug produces a bad cube from a bad pair. This one produces an unusable cube from a good pair, whenever the pair covers a narrow color range, which is the normal case.

## Acceptance criteria

- [x] The cube build weights each cell by its sample count and blends toward identity as confidence falls (for example `identity + n/(n+k) * (mean - identity)`), instead of trusting 1-sample cells fully.
- [x] The cube is smoothed in 3D, and cells far from sampled data fade to identity with a gradual transition, not a cliff. Output stays continuous.
- [x] A generated-image test with a narrow-gamut pair (for example only warm tans and grays) shows bounded cell-to-cell steps in the cube, with a threshold chosen from measurements. The current cube has p99 about 0.55; a derived Look should be far smoother. The test needs no RAW fixture.
- [x] A generated-image test applies the narrow-gamut Look to a smooth green gradient and asserts the result is monotone or has bounded total variation. It must not show banding or a hue flip.
- [ ] Applying the `DSC03293` Look to a foliage photo no longer posterizes. Verify with the real files listed in Fixtures. The `DSC03293` result itself stays close to its JPG, with no visible regression.
- [x] Decide and record why only 3% of the cells received samples with 200k samples. Check whether the edge rejection (`edgeThreshold` 2/255 against a radius-3 blur of a JPG that is 2.7x sharper than the RAW render) rejects most pixels, and whether sampling is dominated by a few colors. Change sampling only if measurements show it helps.
- [x] The Derive sheet makes low coverage visible. For example, below a chosen coverage it says the Look only reflects the colors present in the photo and others fade toward neutral.
- [x] `docs/LOOKS.md` describes how a derived Look behaves outside the sampled colors.

## Implementation notes

- Relevant code: `RecipeExtractor.buildCube`, `accumulateSamples`, `Options` (`smoothingIterations`, `edgeThreshold`, `samplesTarget`), the Derive report view, and `RecipeExtractorTests`, which already drives `buildCube` with hand-built sample distributions.
- `buildCube` is internal for tests. Keep the signature stable or update its tests deliberately.
- A regularized fit (confidence-weighted smoothing toward identity, or a low-order color transform plus residual) is more principled than tuning iteration counts. Pick one and record why.
- Saved or imported Looks are not affected. Do not rewrite existing cubes, per the existing prerelease policy in `docs/LOOKS.md`.
- The sheet and cube names still say `Rec709` although derived cubes are stamped Display P3. See KRMA-1153.

A lone sample keeps under 5% of its offset; populated colors follow their measured mean.
