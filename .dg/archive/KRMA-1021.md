## Objective

Prevent unloaded bulk actions from silently dropping selected IDs when a browsing projection cannot
be resolved.

## Context

`LibraryBrowsingProviding.forEachBrowsingAssetChunk` currently uses `try?` around an entire
`browsingAssets(forIDs:)` chunk and substitutes an empty array on failure. Since that lookup maps
the whole chunk with a throwing operation, one failing asset projection can suppress up to 499
other selected IDs. Culling, clipboard paste, and deletion then appear to succeed with an incomplete
target list and no error message.

## Expected behavior

Isolate lookup failures to the affected IDs and report unresolved targets through the existing
library error/status flow. A partial operation must not be presented as if every selected photo was
processed. Preserve bounded lookup sizes and avoid reading or decoding original image pixels.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] A deterministic test injects one projection failure into a selection larger than 500 IDs and
      proves valid IDs in that chunk and later chunks are still resolved.
- [ ] Culling, paste, and deletion surface unresolved selected IDs and do not claim complete
      success after partial resolution.
- [ ] Lookup remains bounded and uses index metadata without decoding original pixels.
- [ ] `swift build` and `scripts/ci-tests.sh fast` pass.

## Implementation notes

Keep the repair focused on bulk-selection lookup and error reporting. Do not broaden package
identity or index schemas.
