## Objective

Long sessions must not store an unbounded list of scheduler admissions or frame lookups.

## Context

`ImageWorkScheduler.admissionLog` (`Sources/KromoraKit/Models/ImageWorkScheduler.swift`, around line 140) appends every admitted job. The shared `FrameLookupLedger` (`Sources/KromoraKit/Models/Observability.swift`, around line 42) stores every lookup, and its summaries walk the whole list. This is separate from pixel retention (KRMA-999) and from render-key hashing (KRMA-954).

`LiveEditTelemetry.maximumRetainedSamples = 256` (`Sources/KromoraKit/Models/LiveEditTelemetry.swift`, around line 85) is the pattern to copy: cumulative counters plus a fixed ring, or detailed records only inside an explicitly bounded diagnostic session. Parent epic: KRMA-994. Review finding 12.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Admission history and frame-lookup history each have a fixed cap. Counters that tests or logs need (counts by lane, outcome, or kind) still increase after the cap drops old records.
- [ ] Summaries do not scan an ever-growing array. They read the counters and the retained window.
- [ ] Tests fill past the cap and assert the retained count and that the cumulative counter includes the dropped records.
- [ ] Default production construction uses the cap. A test-only unbounded mode is unnecessary.
- [ ] `swift build` is warning-free. `scripts/ci-tests.sh fast` exits 0.
